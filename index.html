Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when using VaultData Studio?

Yes, absolutely. VaultData Studio is built using a local-first architecture. Network requests are completely unnecessary for formatting or masking, ensuring zero data leakage risk.

Why should I mask PII before using AI models?

When feeding raw production logs or user records into AI prompts (like ChatGPT, Claude, or LLMs), customer emails, phone numbers, and real names can easily be logged or used for training. Sanitizing PII protects user privacy and maintains regulatory compliance (GDPR/CCPA).
